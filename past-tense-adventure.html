Stop 1: Learn

Apa itu Past Tense?

Simple Past Tense dipakai untuk menceritakan kegiatan yang sudah selesai di masa lalu.

Waktu belajar: 3 menit
Rumus kalimat positif
Subject
+
Verb 2
+
Past Time
I played football yesterday. Saya bermain sepak bola kemarin.

Time Clues

Cari petunjuk waktu ini:

  • yesterday
  • last night
  • last Sunday
  • two days ago

Positive

S + Verb 2

Rina visited Bali.

Negative

S + did not + Verb 1

Rina did not visit Bali.

Question

Did + S + Verb 1?

Did Rina visit Bali?
